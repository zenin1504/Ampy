Ampy is a lightweight battery info and health estimator, forked from
PlusPlusBattery. It is optimized for Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO devices, and also
works on OnePlus / OPPO / realme and other Android devices via OPlus, Moto,
generic logcat and fallback parsers.

KEY FEATURES
- Real-time dashboard: level, voltage, current, charge/discharge power and
  charts.
- Works without root; root is optional and unlocks extra sysfs fields.
- Battery-health estimation: calculates full-charge capacity when the battery
  is at 100% and current is near zero.
- Cycle-count history stored locally with Room, plus CSV export.
- Persistent battery-monitor notification with customizable metrics.
- Optional floating window.
- Customizable root-mode entries for generic Android power-supply paths.

DEVICE SUPPORT
- Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO: dedicated logcat parsing and monitor recovery.
- OnePlus / OPPO / realme: original PlusPlusBattery parsing retained.
- Other Android devices: generic fallback parsing; available fields depend on
  the device and ROM.

PRIVACY
No ads, no trackers and no internet permission. Data stays on the device.
